Photos are processed entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and your book autosaves on this device as you work. Columns × Rows of 1×1 gives a full page; push either past 1 to split into squares. + Add cover inserts a dedicated front/back cover page with your zine's title on it — the back panel starts pre-flipped 180° since folding mirrors whatever ends up on the outside; nudge it further with its own rotate button if your fold goes the other way. Each page prints at true 6 × 4in / 152.4 × 101.6mm — open Print… and pick your Canon SELPHY (or any AirPrint/USB printer) from the system dialog, scaling off, margins at 0. The primary button hands off to your device's share sheet where supported, which includes Save to Photos.
